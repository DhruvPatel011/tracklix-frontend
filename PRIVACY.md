# Privacy Notice – Tracklix

**Last Updated:** November 2026

Tracklix is an independent personal learning project developed by Dhruv Patel to explore frontend development and personal finance dashboard interfaces.

This notice explains the privacy considerations associated with using the Tracklix demonstration website.

## 1. Purpose of the Project

Tracklix is an educational and experimental project.

It is not a banking service, financial institution, or commercial financial management platform.

The deployed website is provided primarily for demonstration and development purposes.

## 2. Information and Data

Depending on the features currently implemented and enabled, information entered into Tracklix may be:

- Processed within your browser.
- Stored in browser storage, such as Local Storage.
- Sent to the associated backend when a feature makes an API request.
- Stored or processed by the backend if the relevant functionality is implemented.

The exact data handling depends on the current frontend and backend implementation.

This project does not make a blanket guarantee that information entered into the website remains exclusively on your device.

## 3. Browser Storage

Tracklix uses browser-side technologies, including the Local Storage API.

Information stored in browser storage may remain available after closing or reopening the website until it is cleared or removed by the application.

Clearing browser storage may remove locally stored project data.

## 4. Backend and Third-Party Services

Tracklix has a separately maintained backend repository.

Depending on the deployed configuration, requests may be processed by the backend and infrastructure or hosting providers involved in operating the website.

Those services may process technical information as part of providing their infrastructure.

Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

This notice does not claim that all information is stored or processed exclusively by the Tracklix project author.

## 5. Sensitive Information

Please do not enter:

- Real banking usernames or passwords.
- Payment card details.
- Bank account credentials.
- Government identification numbers.
- Sensitive personal or financial information.
- Any information you would not want exposed in an experimental application.

Tracklix is not intended for storing or managing sensitive real-world financial information.

## 6. Data Security

Tracklix is a learning project and is not represented as a security-audited application.

No guarantee is made regarding the security, confidentiality, availability, or integrity of information entered into the website.

Please use the demonstration website accordingly.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data retention depends on where information is stored and which application features are enabled.

- Browser-stored information may be removed through browser storage settings.
- Information stored by a backend may require deletion through the relevant application or backend process.
- Technical logs maintained by hosting or infrastructure providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

Do not assume that clearing browser storage deletes information previously transmitted to a backend.

## 8. Changes to This Notice

This privacy notice may be updated when the project functionality or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will be published in this repository.
